# Results summary

Generated by `python -m lifi run --all`. All numbers are computed, not tuned. ± values are 95% confidence-interval half-widths (Student t) over independent seeds.

## Key findings

- Hybrid delivers 3.3x the Wi-Fi-only throughput (397.9 vs 120.0 Mbps) but 36.7 Mbps less than Li-Fi only, while cutting outage from 9.65% to 7.77%.
- Wi-Fi only is a constant 120 Mbps with 0% outage in every run: its SNR is always above the spectral-efficiency cap (see README, Known issues). All hybrid outage comes from the 0.3 s handover penalty, not from a link failing.
- Hybrid beats Li-Fi only on outage only when Li-Fi is blocked more than about 6% of the time (E4); with little blockage the handover penalty makes the hybrid worse (4.14% vs 0.49% with no blockage).
- Hysteresis gives fewer switches (80 vs 93 / 112 for single thresholds) and the lowest hybrid outage, but not the highest throughput (single 10 dB threshold: 415.7 Mbps vs 397.9) (E7).
- The chosen 15/10 dB thresholds are not throughput-optimal: 10/5 dB gives 426.5 vs 397.9 Mbps (E5). Li-Fi already beats 120 Mbps Wi-Fi above ~4 dB SNR in this model, so high thresholds leave usable Li-Fi unused. Thresholds were NOT retuned for the headline table.
- Bandwidth trades coverage for peak rate: 10 MHz covers most of the room at 15 dB but caps rate at 60 Mbps; 100 MHz covers ~44% (E3). Corner (0,0) is outside the 60 deg FOV by default.
- Four LEDs reach 100% coverage only with 4x the optical power; at equal total power coverage drops to 9.3% (SNR scales with power squared) (E8).
